# Sample FAQ — Acme Store

## About
Acme Store sells home appliances online and delivers across India.

## Support hours
Customer support is available Monday to Saturday, 9 AM to 8 PM India Standard Time. On Sundays we respond by email only.

## Returns and refunds
Products can be returned within 7 days of delivery if unused and in original packaging. Refunds are credited to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days after we receive the item.

## Delivery
Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 business days. Orders above 999 rupees ship free. Express delivery costs 99 rupees extra and arrives in 1 to 2 business days.

## Warranty
All appliances carry a one year manufacturer warranty from the date of purchase.
